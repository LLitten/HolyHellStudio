Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

HolyHell Tools are internal development scripts used by the Lost Faith game team.

What the tools access

When a team member signs in with Google, the tools use the Google Drive API to list, upload and download files in the Drive folders the team uses for game builds and audio packages. They do not read other files, email, contacts or profile data.

Where data is kept

The sign-in token is saved only on the team member's own computer (or as an encrypted secret on the team's build server). Downloaded files stay on that computer. No data is sent to any server run by us or shared with third parties.

Removing access

Delete the saved token file, or remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
