HolyHell Studio is a small team of designers, programmers and artists making Lost Faith. We work across English and Mandarin, so the game, its documents and this site are written in both from the start.

The studio began as a capstone project at RIT. Alongside the five of us at its core, a wider circle of artists, composers and sound designers has helped bring the world of Lost Faith to life.
