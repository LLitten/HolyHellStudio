HolyHell Studio is a small team of designers, programmers, artists and composers making Lost Faith. The team and the project work across multiple languages and countries.

It started with three roommates. Tim, Koa and Zai were already living together before Lost Faith began, and the studio grew out of their capstone project at RIT. Since then it has become a wider team of artists and composers who bring the world of Lost Faith to life.
