## Loot and Playtime

This week's thought is more on the design side. It came to me while planning how often monsters drop loot and materials: I don't think indie or smaller games should stretch out their playtime by turning it into a chore.

In practice, that means avoiding making players repeat the same thing over and over, for example forcing them to grind a level because of low drop rates. Right now the game makes monster drops a little more generous, so players have enough resources to play the way they want.

It comes from where we think the fun is: "how do I build a deck that can beat this dungeon or this enemy?", not "how do I farm my way to a deck?" The first is hard to pull off when resources are too hard to get, and it can even make players quit before they reach the mid and late game.

We're curious what you think. Should indie or smaller games deliberately plan around playtime? Or, as long as it doesn't hurt the fun, should they let players see as much of the game as they can?
