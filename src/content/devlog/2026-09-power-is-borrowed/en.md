In Lost Faith, Vash fights with the power of cards granted by the gods.

But for all their strength, these cards come at a price:

- God cards have durability. When a card's durability reaches zero, it breaks and can't be used in any more battles in that dungeon.
- God cards also affect Vash's mind. Every use raises the meter for that card's faction, pushing Vash toward the edge of sanity.
- As Vash's faith grows, it unlocks extra effects on that faction's cards.

Alternate between cards from different factions to keep the meters in balance, and build a deck that's all your own.

Power is borrowed. Every use comes at a cost.
