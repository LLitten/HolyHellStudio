Windchime Village was once known for its timber, and for a time it was one of the most celebrated logging towns in Razenia. As the two kingdoms tightened their hold on the Pale, its woodcutters put away their axes and went looking for other ways to survive in the wilds.

Then the village was invaded. Followers of Florentis set it ablaze, and to the faithful the screams of the burning sounded like a reward for their devotion. Wind chimes still rattle over the ruined streets.
