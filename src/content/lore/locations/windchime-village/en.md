Windchime Village was once renowned for its bountiful timber, briefly flourishing as a celebrated logging hub across Razenia. Yet as the two kingdoms tightened their restrictions upon the Pale, lumberjacks were forced to put away their axes, seeking other means of survival within the vast wilderness.

When Windchime Village was invaded, followers of Florentis set it ablaze without restraint. The crackle of burning bone and flesh, and the screams of those burned alive, sounded to the faithful like celestial music rewarding their devotion.
