The undisputed heart of the Pale is Denlitin, the region’s largest and most influential trade hub. It took shape some 1,200 years ago, settled mostly by war refugees and people from the borderlands of the two kingdoms, who set aside radical faith to focus on simply surviving.

<!-- Review: "Silent Border agreement" is our own translation of 靜默邊境協議. The team docs have no English name for it yet. -->

About 850 years ago, Denlitin's leaders struck a secret agreement with both kingdoms: trade and economic support in exchange for religious exemption. Some 250 years ago, the two kingdoms signed the Silent Border agreement in Denlitin, agreeing to station consulates there and keep the peace.

Since then, Denlitin has been a high-stakes "neutral zone" where merchant princes rub shoulders with exiled criminals and religious fringe groups. Beneath the surface of commerce, the town is a nest of espionage. Spies from both Rosricon and Belgard walk the streets in plain clothes, monitoring each other and sabotaging any attempt at true Pale unification.
